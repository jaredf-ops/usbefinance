Key financial overview

The USBE Budget had $7.2 billion available and has spent 90.8%. The majority of the remaining $660.1 million is nonlapsing; only $292,055 is scheduled to lapse. The three largest unexpended line item balances represent 67.5% of the total remaining balance, of which the majority of balances are encumbered by grants and contracts.
